WHY LUNA?

From map exploration to mission replanning.

LUNA’s proposed contribution is a workflow that compares multiple site-and-date combinations, exposes changing Sun/Earth geometry, tracks visibility windows, and explains how a schedule or location change affects mission constraints. This is a product direction—not a claim that NASA Moon Trek or QuickMap lacks those capabilities. A fair feature-by-feature benchmark is still required.
